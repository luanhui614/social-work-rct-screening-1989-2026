# Numerical results

`scripts/reproduce.py` regenerates the summary, annual and period rates, journal distributions, conference formats, screening-performance results and linkage summaries from the released records. `scripts/export_review_worklist.py` exports verification membership.

Original model-A category counts describe the source screening extracts and correspond to manuscript Table 1. Final RCT counts follow human verification and adjudication. Linkage summaries use the primary confirmed-same-trial outcome reported in manuscript Figure 4; pair-level relationship codes remain in the released assessment data.
