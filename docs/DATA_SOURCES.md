# Data sources

Bibliographic data and basic variables originate in the [Social Work Meta-Data Project](https://github.com/beperron/SocialWork-MetaData), including the Social Work Research Database and SSWR Conference Database. The study screening extracts contain 65,290 journal records and 23,793 conference records. Extract fingerprints and upstream documentation identifiers are recorded in [the source registry](../data/source_registry.json).

Source variables include record identifiers, title, abstract, author metadata, year, journal or conference format, and DOI. The released study records contain identifiers, years, journal or format, DOI, model classifications, final reference classifications, selection flags and cleaning mappings. Source publication metadata remain linked through their original record IDs.

The studies documenting database construction are:

Perron, B. E., Victor, B. G., & Qi, Z. (2026a). AI-assisted curation of conference scholarship: Compiling, structuring, and analyzing two decades of presentations at the Society for Social Work and Research (2005–2026). *Journal of the Society for Social Work and Research*. Advance online publication. https://doi.org/10.1086/741324

Perron, B. E., Victor, B. G., & Qi, Z. (2026b). Evolution of social work knowledge production over 35 years: An AI-enabled analysis of trends in empiricism, methodology, collaboration, citation patterns, and output. *Research on Social Work Practice, 36*(8), 1576–1590. https://doi.org/10.1177/10497315261416833
